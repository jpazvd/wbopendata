Dear Editors,

I am pleased to resubmit the revised manuscript, "wbopendata: Programmatic Access
to World Bank Open Data," for consideration by the Stata Journal.

This resubmission addresses the editorial feedback received on February 23, 2026,
regarding ado-file naming conventions. All internal ado-files that previously
used single-underscore prefixes (e.g., _query.ado, _website.ado) have been
renamed to use the double-underscore __wbod_ prefix pattern (e.g., __wbod_query.ado,
__wbod_website.ado) to avoid potential conflicts with official Stata routines.

The paper documents wbopendata, a community-contributed Stata command that has
provided programmatic access to World Bank Open Data since 2011. With over
150,000 installations on SSC, wbopendata enables researchers to download more
than 29,000 indicators from 71 World Bank databases spanning 296 countries and
aggregates. The paper advances the argument that treating data acquisition as
code---where indicator selections, country filters, and time ranges become
explicit parameters in analysis scripts---offers a practical response to the data
provenance dimension of the reproducibility crisis.

The paper documents the current version (18.4), including:

  - Five download modes with multilingual metadata support
  - A YAML-backed offline metadata architecture for indicator discovery
  - Version-controlled metadata synchronization
  - An 89-scenario automated test suite
  - Three design principles distilled from fifteen years of sustained use

All manuscript, code, and help file references have been updated to reflect the
new naming convention.

The submission includes the complete software package (ado-files, help files,
YAML metadata, and support files), a do-file reproducing all paper examples,
and the corresponding log output.

This paper has not been previously published and is not under review elsewhere.

I am happy to provide any additional materials the editors or reviewers may
require.

Thank you for considering this submission.
